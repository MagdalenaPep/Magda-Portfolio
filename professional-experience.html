What I do

I work end to end: interpreting client requirements, defining performance metrics, transforming the data, building scalable data models, developing dashboards, running quality assurance and writing the documentation.

The challenge is to bring complex data, bespoke calculations and several levels of reporting into one intuitive experience, so people can analyse detail and make high-level decisions from the same source.

My contribution

  • Requirements and KPIs: interpreted client briefs and defined the metrics, targets and benchmarks.
  • Data engineering: wrote SQL (CTEs, window functions, table creation) in Snowflake and supported scalable transformations with dbt and Python.
  • Dashboards: modelled the data in the BI layer and designed the reporting for each audience.
  • Data quality tooling: designed and built an internal, self-serve tool for spotting data issues, combining parameterised SQL, a BI dashboard and an AI agent.
  • Quality assurance: validated my own work, tested with colleagues, resolved discrepancies and acted on client feedback.
  • Documentation: wrote user guides explaining KPI definitions, calculations and how to read the results.

Because of confidentiality and intellectual property agreements, this page describes my responsibilities and skills only. It does not include client names, data, screenshots, internal designs or results.

Areas of work

Dashboard development

Dashboards for brand, category, e-commerce and executive audiences, from global overviews to detailed local views. I decide on information hierarchy, filters, labels, navigation and performance, so depth doesn’t overwhelm the user.

KPI design and business logic

Turning client objectives into metrics: targets and benchmarks, weighted measures, custom mappings, bespoke category hierarchies and combined performance scores.

Data quality tooling

A self-serve tool that helps teams spot a suspected data issue, using parameterised SQL, a BI dashboard and an AI agent that guides them from detection to raising a ticket with the right owner.

Skills in practice

SQL & Snowflake

CTEs, window functions, table creation and queries written to perform at scale.

Data modelling

Star-schema and composite-key joins at a daily grain, so metrics are not double-counted and can be trended and drilled into.

Aggregation techniques

Conditional aggregation to pivot row states into columns, and distinct counting of unique entities rather than raw rows.

Parameterised queries

One template reused across client accounts, date ranges and filters.

dbt & Python

Freshness and lineage checks
