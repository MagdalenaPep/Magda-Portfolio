Executive KPI Reporting & Dashboard Development

I turn ambiguous commercial requirements into bespoke, scalable and well-documented BI solutions: complex e-commerce data, shaped into actionable insight for brand managers, e-commerce teams and executives.

RoleEnd to end, from requirements to handover
Built withSnowflake, SQL, dbt, Python, BI dashboards
AudienceBrand, category, e-commerce and executive teams

Project overview

Each solution is tailored to the client’s commercial objectives and reporting needs. I work end to end: interpreting client requirements, defining performance metrics, transforming the data, building scalable data models, developing the dashboards, running quality assurance and writing the documentation.

The challenge is to bring complex data, bespoke calculations and multiple levels of reporting into a single, intuitive experience that supports both detailed performance analysis and high-level strategic decisions.

My contribution

  • Requirements and KPIs: interpreted client briefs and defined the metrics, targets and benchmarks.
  • Data engineering: wrote the SQL (CTEs, window functions, table creation) in Snowflake and supported scalable transformations with dbt and Python.
  • Dashboards: modelled the data in the BI layer and designed the reporting for each audience.
  • Quality assurance: validated my own work, tested with colleagues, resolved discrepancies and acted on client feedback.
  • Documentation: wrote the user guides explaining KPI definitions, calculations and how to read the results.

Client names, data and company-specific terminology are anonymised throughout this page.

The business challenge

E-commerce businesses need to understand how their brands and categories perform at many levels, from individual retailers and markets up to global performance and executive KPIs. Different stakeholders need different perspectives on the same underlying data.

Brand and category managers need detail to monitor performance and spot opportunities. E-commerce managers and executives need consistent, consolidated metrics to judge progress against targets. Delivering all of this in one reporting solution is the hard part:

Multiple stakeholder requirements

Supporting local teams, global teams and executive audiences within one cohesive reporting framework.

Complex metric definitions

Translating commercial objectives into meaningful KPIs, benchmarks, weighted metrics and bespoke performance scores.

Client-specific business logic

Custom brand mappings, retailer-defined keywords, bespoke category structures and tailored methods of analysing performance.

Complex e-commerce data

Accounting for the nuances of different retailers, data sources, metrics and reporting requirements.

Scalability and performance

Data models that support complex calculations and efficient dashboards across multiple client accounts.

Strict delivery timelines

Managing scope, technical feasibility, development, testing and stakeholder feedback against agreed deadlines.

KPI design and bespoke business logic

A key part of the work is translating client-specific objectives into metrics that accurately represent performance. Rather than relying only on standard e-commerce indicators, the reporting can include bespoke calculations, weighting methodologies, benchmark definitions and custom data mappings that reflect each client’s priorities.

Performance targets and benchmarks

Defining appropriate targets and comparison criteria for different metrics, retailers, brands or categories.

Custom brand mapping

Mapping retailer-defined keywords and product terminology to the client’s brand structure.

Bespoke category hierarchies

Custom category groupings, so performance can be analysed using the client’s own business definitions.

Weighted performance metrics

Tailored weighting methodologies that reflect the relative importance of individual KPIs.

Holistic performance scoring

Multiple e-commerce indicators combined into one consolidated score for a broader view of performance.

These requirements take analytical thinking, commercial understanding and technical problem-solving, so that the resulting metrics are meaningful, consistent and fit for purpose.

Technical approach

SQL and Snowflake transform complex source data into structured reporting datasets, using common table expressions, window functions, table creation and bespoke calculation logic.

dbt and Python support the development and scaling of that logic across multiple client accounts, keeping it consistent while allowing for client-specific requirements.

In the BI layer, the datasets are modelled further and organised to support efficient reporting, intuitive navigation and consistent KPI presentation across stakeholder groups.

Dashboard design and user experience

Good dashboard design is more than choosing charts. It takes decisions about information hierarchy, labels, legends, slicers, tooltips or pop-outs, colour, layout, navigation and performance. The central challenge is providing depth without overwhelming the user.

Quality assurance and documentation

Data and calculation validation

Checking that SQL outputs, KPI calculations, mappings, totals and dashboard results align with the agreed business definitions.

Collaborative QA and iteration

Testing with colleagues, resolving defects and refining the solution based on QA findings and client feedback.

Detailed user documentation

Documenting KPI definitions, complex calculations, filters, dashboard functionality and interpretation guidance, so users can work confidently with the reporting.

Business impact

The resulting reporting solutions give clients a consistent framework for evaluating e-commerce performance, while meeting the different needs of global, local and executive stakeholders.

By combining bespoke KPI logic, scalable data transformations and carefully designed visualisations, the dashboards support performance monitoring, comparison against targets and better-informed decisions.

The end-to-end approach also puts data reliability, maintainability and usability first, through extensive QA, iterative refinement and detailed documentation.
