About me

I'm Magdalena, a data analyst based in London and Watford. I'm at my best when I'm transforming complex, fragmented data into something people can act on, whether that's a quick answer for a team today or a reporting tool that keeps working long after I've built it. In my current role, I build, support and manage 15+ dashboards, working in Agile sprints alongside my team in a fast-paced, quickly changing e-commerce environment.

I graduated with a First-Class BSc in Mathematics from Queen Mary University of London and joined Profitero+ as a Graduate Analyst in 2023, moving up to Data Analyst in 2025. Since then I've worked across SQL, Snowflake and BigQuery, migrated client reporting from Power BI to Sigma Computing, and helped move dashboard data to Snowflake. I'm now building Snowflake Agents alongside AI engineers, so clients can explore their own data.

I also bring a quality mindset to analysis. Smoke and sanity testing, QA checks and a UX-led approach to design ensure the figures are accurate and the people using them can navigate them with ease.
